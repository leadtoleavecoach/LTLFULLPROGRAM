Your Big Uncomfortable Goal is the one leadership habit you avoid — that, if you built it, would change everything. Find it below.

1 · Catch it

List what you put off, hand off, or quietly hope sorts itself out. Go fast — don’t edit.

2 · Narrow it

3 · Make it granular

Rewrite it as a specific action, in a specific moment. (Too broad: “I avoid conflict.” Granular: “When someone’s attitude is hurting the team, I avoid naming it to their face the same day.”)
