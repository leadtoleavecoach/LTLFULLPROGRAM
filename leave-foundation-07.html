The last work is internal. The goal was never to be needed — it was to be missed. Letting go is an identity shift: three flips — Hero → Builder, Control → Trust, Present → Permanent. The daily proof you've flipped is that you go last.

1 · Catch the pull

The last time you jumped into work your team could have handled — what were you really getting from it? Name the feeling. (Needed? The one with the answer?) You can't flip what you won't admit.

2 · Find your hardest flip

Which flip is hardest for you — and why does it have a grip?

3 · The empty-chair test

Picture your operation running a full month without you in it. What's the first feeling — pride, or fear? The honest answer tells you how much of your identity is still tied to being needed.

4 · Pick a go-last move

One specific way you'll go last this week — give away a win that was yours, take a task nobody wants, or let someone else have the answer in a room where you knew it.

5 · Write the reframe

The mindset, compressed. Put it somewhere you'll see it.

My job is no longer to be the one who…It's to build people who…

Reflect

If your team ran beautifully for a month without you, would you feel proud or threatened? That feeling is the last thing between you and the finish line.

Who are you when you're not needed?

Your rep
