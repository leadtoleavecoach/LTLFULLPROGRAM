Foundation 1
Watch first
Then work the pages below
Leadership is influence that multiplies — not your title, not your output. Run the High-Potential read on every person: Results · Attitude · Hunger. All three = trajectory. Build them first.

1 · Name them all

List every person on your team. Fast — the ones who jump to mind and the ones you almost forgot.

2 · Score each — Results · Attitude · Hunger

Tap H (high), M (mid), or L (low) for each signal. First instinct is usually the honest one.

NameResultsAttitudeHunger

3 · Circle the threes

Who scored High on all three? Those are your build-first people. There won't be many — there shouldn't be.

4 · Write the proof

For each build-first person, the one specific thing you've actually seen — not "great attitude." Proof, not praise.

5 · Set the date

Who you'll tell — to their face — what you see, and when. This week.

PersonWhen I'll tell them

Notice the gap

How many of your "best people" are Results-only — strong producers who've plateaued? That gap is the trap this Foundation breaks.

Reflect

Who did you almost leave off the list because they're quiet — and is that a potential problem, or just a visibility problem?

Who have you been over-investing in because they remind you of you, not because they actually have all three?

Your rep

Before the next Foundation, take one person you circled and tell them — specifically — what you see in them. Then write down what happened: not whether it was perfect, just what happened and how they reacted.
