Stage 3 · LEAVE — Foundation 03
Paint the Picture
Foundation 3
Watch first
Then work the pages below
People don't self-direct toward a task — they self-direct toward a picture. Write The Postcard: a specific future, in present tense, vivid enough that your people can see it the same way you do. Specific · them in it · a why. Then say it till you're sick of it — and say it again.

1 · Pick the date

A specific point in the future — start one year out. A real date makes it concrete instead of "someday."

2 · Write the day

Present tense. One normal day at that future point — what the team does without you driving it, what they can handle now that they couldn't before. Write a scene, not goals.

3 · Add the voices

Real words, the way they'd actually say them.

A customer says…A team member says…

4 · Name the why

One line: who is better off because this picture became real? Not the revenue — the human on the other end.

5 · The one-breath version

Compress the whole Postcard into one or two sentences you could say out loud in a hallway today. This is the version you'll repeat until you're sick of it.

Reflect

If you pulled three people aside separately and asked where this team is headed, would you get the same answer three times?

Does your vision give people a reason, or just a target?

Your rep

Say your one-breath Postcard out loud to your team this week, where they're together. Then write what you saw — did it land? Did anyone repeat it back?
