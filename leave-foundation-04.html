Foundation 4

Then work the pages below
Autonomy is a map, not a vibe. Every decision sits in one of three lanes — Decide (theirs, no need to tell you), Tell (decide, then tell you after), Ask (bring it to you first). Keep the Ask lane small. Then move people up a lane over time as they prove out.

1 · Pick the person

One person you need to own more than they currently do.

2–3 · List the decisions & sort the lanes

The recurring calls in their world. Tap the lane each one lives in today — honest about how it works now, not how you wish it worked.

Recurring decisionLane (today)

4 · Promote one

One decision this person is ready to move up — a Red(Ask)→Yellow(Tell), or a Yellow(Tell)→Green(Decide). This is your move this week.

5 · Write the words

Exactly how you'll tell them the lane changed. A lane nobody announced is a lane nobody trusts.

Reflect

What's the decision your team interrupts you for most often? That's almost always a decision that should already be in someone's green lane. Why is it still yours?

Do you hold certain decisions because they're genuinely high-stakes — or because being the one who decides is part of how you feel important?

Your rep

Pass the one key you marked to promote, this week — out loud, in the words you wrote. Then write what happened: did they take it and run, or keep coming back to you?
